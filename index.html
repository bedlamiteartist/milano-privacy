Milano Site Check — Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 28, 2026

Milano Site Check ("the App") is operated by Milano Doors & Mouldings. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account or use the App, we may collect:

  • Account information — your name and email address when you sign up
  • Inspection data — customer names, site addresses, door measurements, and material quantities you enter during inspections
  • Authentication data — if you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your name and email from those providers

We do not collect payment information, precise GPS location, or device contacts.

2. Microphone Access

The App requests access to your device microphone solely to enable voice input for notes and comments within the inspection form. Audio is processed on-device by your operating system's speech recognition service and is never recorded or stored by Milano Doors & Mouldings.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and maintain your account
  • To store and sync your inspection drafts across your devices
  • To generate and email PDF inspection reports to the designated supplier
  • To improve the App and troubleshoot issues

We do not sell your data to third parties and do not use your data for advertising.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your account and inspection data is stored securely using Supabase (supabase.com), a cloud database provider. Data is encrypted in transit using TLS and at rest. Inspection reports are emailed via Resend (resend.com).

Lock codes entered in the App are stored on your device only and are never uploaded to our servers.

5. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Supabase — authentication and data storage
  • Resend — email delivery
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple
  • Google — Sign in with Google

6. Data Retention

Your account and inspection data is retained for as long as your account is active. Submitted inspections are retained for record-keeping purposes. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

7. Guest Mode

You may use the App as a guest without creating an account. In guest mode, all data is stored locally on your device only and is not synced to our servers. Guest sessions expire after 7 days of inactivity.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Withdraw consent at any time by deleting the App

9. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for use by adults (18+) in a professional capacity. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes via the App. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
