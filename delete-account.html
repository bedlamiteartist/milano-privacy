Milano Site Check — Delete Account

Request Account Deletion

You can request the deletion of your Milano Site Check account and all associated data at any time by following the steps below.

How to request deletion

1
Send an email to our support address with the subject line: "Account Deletion Request"
2
Include the email address associated with your Milano Site Check account so we can locate your data.
3
We will confirm the deletion and process your request within 7 business days.
📧 Send your request to: info@milanodoors.ca

What gets deleted

  • Your account credentials (email, name, password)
  • All inspection drafts and job data synced to our servers
  • Your authentication tokens and session data

What is retained

  • Submitted inspection records may be retained for up to 90 days for business record-keeping purposes, after which they are permanently deleted
  • Data stored locally on your device is not affected — uninstalling the app will remove it

In-app deletion

You can also sign out and stop using the app at any time. To remove locally stored data immediately, simply uninstall the app from your device.
